Questions, answered

Is this a real phone call?

Yes — in production the agent dials the buyer's real number. This demo uses a simulated phone inside the demo store, so you can try the full voice loop in your browser with no telephony setup.

What languages does it speak?

Hindi, Hinglish, English, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam — the agent detects the customer's language from their first reply and switches automatically.

What if the buyer doesn't pick up?

VerifyCOD retries on your schedule (default: 3 attempts, 30 minutes apart), then marks the order for review. You can also fall back to WhatsApp or SMS after the final attempt.

Does it ask for OTPs or payment details?

Never. The agent is hard-blocked from asking for OTPs, PINs, card numbers or UPI details, and it discloses that it's an automated assistant at the start of every call.

What happens to call recordings?

Audio and transcripts are stored encrypted, auto-deleted after your retention period (default 90 days), and never used to train models. DPDP-compliant by design.
