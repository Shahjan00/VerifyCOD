Questions, answered

Is this a real phone call?

Yes. In production the agent dials the buyer's real number. The demo uses a simulated phone so you can try the full voice loop in your browser.

What languages does it speak?

Hindi, Hinglish, English, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam. It detects the customer's language from their first reply and switches automatically.

What if the buyer doesn't pick up?

VerifyCOD retries on your schedule (default: 3 attempts, 30 minutes apart), then marks the order for review.

Does it ask for OTPs or payment details?

Never. The agent is blocked from asking for OTPs, PINs, card numbers or UPI details, and it says it's an automated assistant at the start of every call.

What happens to call recordings?

Audio and transcripts are stored encrypted, deleted after your retention period (default 90 days) and never used to train models.
